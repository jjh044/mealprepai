# PrepWise Creator Partner Information Sheet

Prepared for prospective creator partners

## Quick Overview

PrepWise Meal Planner helps people plan meals, find recipe ideas, organize grocery lists, and make weekly meal prep easier around their goals, schedule, and budget.

The creator partner program is designed for meal prep, family meal planning, nutrition, healthy lifestyle, budget grocery, and fitness-food creators who want to earn from referred paid subscribers instead of doing only one-time sponsored posts.

Public partner page: https://www.prepwiseai.app/partners

## How The Revenue Share Works

Each approved creator partner receives a unique referral link or creator code.

When someone signs up for a paid PrepWise subscription through that link or code, the creator earns a percentage of the subscription revenue tied to that customer.

This is intended to be recurring revenue share: when a referred customer remains an active paid subscriber, the creator can continue receiving partner payments tied to that customer's eligible subscription revenue rather than only receiving a one-time payment.

For founding partners, the current proposed structure is:

- 20% revenue share on referred paid subscriptions
- Tracking through a unique partner link or creator code
- Payouts based on confirmed paid subscription revenue from referred customers
- No upfront fee or required ad spend from the creator
- No customer support, app development, or billing setup required from the creator

Final commission terms, attribution rules, payout timing, and refund handling will be confirmed in writing before any campaign goes live.

## Why This Can Fit Your Audience

PrepWise is built for people who want meal planning help without starting from scratch every week.

Your audience may be a good fit if they care about:

- Saving time on weekly meal planning
- Getting meal prep ideas quickly
- Staying consistent with health, fitness, or family food goals
- Creating grocery lists faster
- Planning around budget, store choices, and household needs
- Turning creator-style meal ideas into a practical weekly plan

## What You Would Promote

Creators can promote PrepWise in a natural, useful way by showing how the app fits into real meal planning.

Example content angles:

- "I used PrepWise to build a 5-day meal prep plan."
- "Here is how I plan a high-protein grocery week with PrepWise."
- "I challenged PrepWise to create meals around my grocery budget."
- "Busy week meal planning: what the app made vs. what I cooked."
- "Family meal plan or freezer prep week using PrepWise."

The goal is useful content first, with the partner link or code included as the next step for followers who want to try the app.

## What PrepWise Provides

PrepWise will provide:

- A unique referral link or creator code
- Basic tracking for referred signups and paid subscriptions
- App access for content planning and testing
- Suggested talking points and content ideas if helpful
- Direct founder contact for launch questions and feedback

## What We Ask From Creators

We are looking for creators who can:

- Try the app before promoting it
- Share honest, practical content with their audience
- Use their unique link or code when promoting PrepWise
- Let us know what questions or objections their audience has
- Start with one small content test before deciding whether to expand

There is no requirement to make a large campaign commitment upfront.

## Suggested First Test

A simple first test could be one short-form video, one story sequence, one email mention, or one YouTube segment that shows PrepWise being used in a real planning scenario.

After the first test, we can review:

- Link clicks
- Trial or signup activity
- Paid subscription conversions
- Audience questions
- Whether a second piece of content makes sense

## Important Notes

This information sheet is meant to explain the program at a high level. It is not a final contract.

Any official partnership will be confirmed in writing before launch, including:

- Revenue-share percentage
- How long commissions apply
- Tracking and attribution rules
- Payout schedule
- Refund or cancellation handling
- Any content usage permissions
