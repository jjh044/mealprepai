PrepWise Privacy Policy

PrepWise helps users create meal plans, grocery lists, preparation guidance, and estimated store comparisons.

Information we process

PrepWise may process ZIP code, household size, budget, dietary preferences, saved meal plans, favorites, account identifiers, subscription and purchase status, support messages, and device or diagnostic information.

How information is used

Information is used to generate meal plans, personalize recipes, create grocery lists, provide subscription access, restore purchases, respond to support requests, protect the service, and improve reliability.

Service providers

Meal and ingredient information may be sent to OpenAI for AI-generated preparation guidance. Official YouTube Data API metadata may be used to find public cooking videos and link users to the original creator. Recipe, video, nutrition, mapping, store, and retailer requests may also be processed by Spoonacular, Tasty, Edamam, Google Maps or Places, Instacart, RapidAPI, Vercel, Apple, and other configured infrastructure providers.

Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. PrepWise receives transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription state, renewal state, expiration information, and refund or revocation events needed to provide paid access. PrepWise does not receive full payment-card details.

Analytics and diagnostics

The current build does not include third-party advertising. Production builds may collect limited crash reports, performance diagnostics, and feature usage analytics. Any enabled provider will be disclosed in App Store privacy details and this policy.

Retention and deletion

Local guest data remains on the device until removed by the user. Production account data will be retained while the account is active and for limited periods needed for security, legal, fraud-prevention, tax, and transaction-record obligations. Users can initiate deletion from Account in the app.

Your choices

Users can change preferences, withdraw optional permissions in device settings, cancel subscriptions in Apple settings, sign out, or delete their account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Nutrition and allergens

Nutrition, allergens, ingredients, costs, and retailer information are estimates and are not medical advice. Always verify labels and consult an appropriate professional for medical or dietary needs.
