# PrepWise Creator Partnership Agreement

## 1. Partnership Overview

PrepWise and Creator are entering into a simple referral partnership. Creator may share PrepWise with their audience using an approved referral link, creator code, or other tracking method provided by PrepWise.

Creator is not required to make a large campaign commitment, post on a fixed schedule, or purchase advertising. The goal is honest, useful content that helps Creator's audience decide whether PrepWise is a good fit for them.

## 2. Revenue Share Percentage

Creator will receive 20% of net subscription revenue received by PrepWise from customers who subscribe through Creator's approved referral link, creator code, or other approved tracking method.

"Net subscription revenue" means the subscription revenue actually received by PrepWise after app store commissions, payment processing fees if applicable, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and similar deductions.

Commissions apply to eligible referred customers for as long as those customers remain active paid subscribers, unless this Agreement is terminated under Section 7.

## 3. Payment Schedule

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid by the 15th day of the following month.

For example, eligible July earnings would be paid by August 15.

The minimum payout amount is $25. If Creator's earned commission for a month is less than $25, the balance will roll over to the next month until the minimum payout amount is reached.

Payments will be made using the payment method agreed to by PrepWise and Creator. Creator is responsible for providing accurate payment information and any tax information reasonably requested for payment processing.

## 4. Referral Tracking

PrepWise will use its referral tracking system to determine eligible referrals, subscriptions, and commissions.

Referral attribution is based on PrepWise's records and will be considered final in the event of discrepancies.

To help ensure accurate tracking, Creator agrees to use only the referral link, creator code, or tracking method provided or approved by PrepWise. PrepWise is not responsible for missed commissions caused by altered links, incorrect codes, platform tracking limits, user privacy settings, failed payments, refunds, chargebacks, or purchases that are not attributed in PrepWise's tracking system.

## 5. Partnership Terms

This partnership begins on the effective date and continues until terminated by either party.

Creator is an independent contractor and is not an employee, agent, legal representative, joint venturer, or partner of PrepWise. Creator is responsible for their own taxes, equipment, content production costs, and business expenses.

PrepWise may provide Creator with app access, talking points, brand information, or content ideas. Creator is not required to use exact scripts unless both parties agree in writing.

## 6. Content Guidelines

Creator agrees to:

- Represent PrepWise honestly and in good faith.
- Share only truthful statements about PrepWise and their own experience with the app.
- Avoid false, misleading, exaggerated, or unsupported claims.
- Follow applicable laws, platform rules, and FTC disclosure requirements.
- Clearly disclose the partnership or affiliate relationship when required, using clear language such as "affiliate link," "ad," "paid partnership," or similar wording.
- Avoid content that could reasonably damage the reputation, trust, or goodwill of PrepWise.

Creator retains full creative control over how PrepWise is presented, provided the content remains truthful, respectful, and compliant with applicable laws and platform policies.

PrepWise may ask Creator to correct, edit, or remove content that contains inaccurate claims, missing disclosures, improper use of PrepWise branding, or content that could reasonably harm PrepWise.

## 7. Termination Policy

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with 14 days' written notice.

PrepWise may terminate this Agreement immediately if Creator makes false or misleading claims, fails to disclose the partnership when required, misuses the referral system, engages in fraudulent activity, or publishes content that materially harms PrepWise.

Commissions earned before termination will still be paid according to the payment schedule, subject to refund, chargeback, tax, and payment processing adjustments.

After termination, Creator will not earn commissions from new subscriptions created after the termination date.

Existing referred subscribers will remain commissionable for as long as they remain active paid subscribers, unless the Agreement was terminated for fraud, misleading claims, failure to disclose, misuse of the referral system, or material harm to PrepWise.

## 8. Confidentiality

If PrepWise shares non-public information with Creator, Creator agrees not to disclose that information without PrepWise's written permission.

Confidential information may include private product plans, unreleased features, referral performance data, payment details, customer information, internal business information, or other information marked or reasonably understood as confidential.

## 9. Brand Use

Creator may use PrepWise's name, app screenshots, approved links, and approved brand assets only for content related to this partnership.

Creator may not modify PrepWise logos, imply ownership of PrepWise, or suggest that PrepWise sponsors unrelated products, services, or claims without written permission.

## 10. Content License for Marketing and Paid Advertising

During the term of this Agreement, Creator grants PrepWise a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, edit, crop, resize, caption, and distribute content created by Creator that promotes PrepWise for PrepWise marketing and advertising purposes.

This includes use across digital platforms and paid advertising channels, including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and similar services.

PrepWise may make reasonable edits to Creator's content, provided the overall message and endorsement are not materially changed.

Creator understands that paid advertising campaigns funded by PrepWise may use PrepWise's own tracking links, landing pages, or campaign tracking methods rather than Creator's referral link, because PrepWise is responsible for the cost and risk of those advertising campaigns.

Whenever reasonably practical, PrepWise will keep Creator's name, username, logo, or other branding in the advertisement to help support Creator's visibility and recognition.

For paid advertising campaigns funded by PrepWise that use Creator's content, Creator is eligible to receive a $100 performance bonus for every 100 paid subscriptions generated by that advertisement, based on PrepWise's tracking records.

PrepWise may also, at its sole discretion, offer additional performance-based bonuses for paid advertising campaigns that use Creator's content. Any additional bonus amount, eligibility requirement, or performance milestone, including subscription or customer acquisition goals, must be communicated to Creator in writing before becoming effective.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, any performance-based bonus is separate from and in addition to any referral commissions earned under this Agreement.

The rights granted under this section automatically end when this Agreement is terminated, except that PrepWise may continue running advertising campaigns that are already live for up to 30 days after termination solely to allow an orderly wind-down of active campaigns.

## 11. No Guaranteed Results

PrepWise does not guarantee that Creator will earn any specific amount. Earnings depend on tracked referrals, active paid subscriptions, refunds, cancellations, platform fees, taxes, and other adjustments.

## 12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the complete agreement between PrepWise and Creator for this referral partnership and replaces any prior discussions about the same subject. Any changes must be agreed to in writing by both parties.
