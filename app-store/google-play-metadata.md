## Full description

PrepWise turns a few simple choices into a focused five-day meal-prep plan.

Set your weekly budget, household size, ZIP code, and food preference. PrepWise selects breakfast, lunch, and dinner ideas, combines their ingredients into one organized grocery list, and shows estimated nearby-store comparisons.

FREE FEATURES

• Build a limited number of weekly meal plans
• Choose balanced, high-protein, vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free preferences
• Generate an organized grocery list
• Swap a limited number of meals
• Use limited AI-assisted preparation guidance

PREPWISE PRO

• Unlimited meal-plan builds
• Unlimited meal swaps
• Unlimited AI prep tips and recipe instructions
• Saved plan history
• Subscription access across supported signed-in devices

IMPORTANT

Nutrition, allergens, ingredients, costs, availability, and store totals are estimates. Always verify food labels, allergens, preparation safety, and current retailer prices. PrepWise does not provide medical advice.

PrepWise Pro is available as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription. Prices and any trial terms are shown before purchase. Manage or cancel through your Google Play subscription settings.
