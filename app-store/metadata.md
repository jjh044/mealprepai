## Description

PrepWise turns a few simple choices into a focused five-day meal-prep plan.

Set your weekly budget, household size, ZIP code, and food preference. PrepWise selects breakfast, lunch, and dinner ideas, combines their ingredients into one grocery list, and shows estimated nearby-store comparisons.

FREE FEATURES

- Build a limited number of weekly meal plans
- Choose balanced, high-protein, vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free preferences
- Generate an organized grocery list
- Swap a limited number of meals
- Use limited AI-assisted preparation guidance

PREPWISE PRO

- Unlimited meal-plan builds
- Unlimited meal swaps
- Unlimited AI prep tips and recipe instructions
- Saved plan history
- Subscription access across supported signed-in devices when cloud accounts launch

IMPORTANT
