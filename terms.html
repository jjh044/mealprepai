PrepWise Terms of Use

PrepWise provides meal-planning and grocery-organization tools for general informational use.

Subscriptions

PrepWise Pro plans are auto-renewing subscriptions. On the web, PrepWise Pro is offered at $9.99 per month with a seven-day trial and $69.99 per year without a trial, unless a different localized price or promotion is displayed at checkout. Stripe processes web billing. App Store prices and trial terms are displayed by Apple before purchase.

Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage web subscriptions through the Stripe customer portal and App Store subscriptions through Apple. Deleting a web account cancels its linked Stripe customer relationship. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
