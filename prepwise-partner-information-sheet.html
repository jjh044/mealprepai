PrepWise Partner Information Sheet

Welcome!

Thank you for your interest in partnering with PrepWise.

PrepWise was built to make meal planning simple, affordable, and less stressful. Instead of spending hours searching for recipes, users can quickly discover meals, build grocery lists, estimate grocery costs, and stay within their food budget all in one place.

Our goal is to help people eat better while saving both time and money.

Why We're Looking for Creators

We believe the best recommendations come from people who genuinely use and enjoy products.

Rather than paying for one-time sponsored posts, we want to build long-term partnerships with creators whose audiences can truly benefit from PrepWise.

If your content focuses on meal prep, grocery budgeting, healthy eating, family meals, fitness nutrition, or everyday cooking, we think this could be a great fit.

How the Partnership Works

When you become a PrepWise Partner, you'll receive:

  • Your own unique referral link
  • Revenue sharing for every qualifying subscription purchased through your link
  • Access to new features and updates as they're released
  • A direct line of communication with our team

There are no quotas or required posting schedules. We simply ask that any content featuring PrepWise is honest and authentic to your audience.

Revenue Share

As a PrepWise Partner, you'll receive 20% of revenue based on PrepWise Meal Planner's net subscription revenue from qualifying customers who subscribe through your unique referral link.

This is recurring revenue share: when someone signs up through your referral link and remains an active paid subscriber, you can continue receiving partner payments tied to that customer's eligible subscription revenue, rather than only receiving a one-time payment.

Revenue Share 20%

Net subscription revenue means the subscription amount PrepWise receives after app store platform fees, payment processing fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, and similar adjustments.

Partners should expect to receive roughly $1.70 per qualifying subscription after platform fees, subject to final subscription price, taxes, refunds, and other adjustments.

Your referral link allows us to track subscriptions and attribute them to you.

Payments

  • Payments are issued monthly.
  • Earnings are tracked through your referral link.
  • Additional payment details will be included in the partnership agreement.
