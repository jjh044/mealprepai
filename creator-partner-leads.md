## Outreach Angle

Offer each creator a simple creator-code campaign:

- "Build my 5-day PrepWise plan using my weekly grocery budget."
- "Use my creator code for PrepWise Pro."
- "I challenged PrepWise to make a high-protein week under $X."
- "Busy mom freezer-prep week: app-generated plan vs my real grocery run."
- "Aldi/Walmart/Costco budget meal prep using PrepWise."

Avoid promising fixed revenue-share percentages until tracking, payout timing, refund handling, and subscription attribution rules are finalized.
